Booking and seat safety

  • Customers see departures that have been added and are still active.
  • Pending seat requests are temporarily hidden from other customers.
  • Confirming a request reserves its seats. Rejecting it makes them available again.
  • Share the spreadsheet only with staff who are permitted to see customer contact details.
